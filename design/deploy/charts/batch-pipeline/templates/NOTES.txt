{{ .Chart.Name }}-{{ .Release.Name }} 已部署到集群。

1. 获取 Pod 状态:
   kubectl get pods -l "app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}" -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. 获取 Service 信息:
   kubectl get svc {{ include "batch-pipeline.serviceName" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

3. 探活:
   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "batch-pipeline.serviceName" . }} 8096:8096 -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
   curl http://127.0.0.1:8086/health
